How WalkSync works

Finding a walking partner is simple.

  1. Choose your area

    Enter your locality and the time of day you like to walk.

  2. Pick your preference

    Filter by partner preference and browse short, friendly profiles.

  3. Connect & walk

    Send a request. Once they accept, you can share contact details and meet.

Your safety matters

WalkSync helps people connect for walking. A few habits keep every first meeting comfortable.

Meet in public

Choose a busy, well-lit spot such as a park entrance or market for the first walk.

Tell someone

Share your plan, route and partner's name with a friend or family member.

Protect your details

Contact details are shared only after a request is accepted. Share more only when you're ready.

Trust your instincts

If something feels off, leave. You can decline or cancel any request at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is WalkSync free?

Yes. Creating an account, listing yourself and sending walk requests are free.

How do I become a walking partner?

Create an account, choose "List yourself", and add your locality, preferred time, pace and a short bio. You can pause or remove your listing whenever you like.

When are my contact details shared?

Only after you accept a request (or someone accepts yours). Until then, other members only see your first name, locality and walking preferences.

Can I cancel or decline a request?

Absolutely. Pending requests you sent can be cancelled, and requests you receive can be accepted or declined from the "My walks" panel.
